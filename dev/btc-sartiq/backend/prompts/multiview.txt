MULTI-VIEW REFERENCE RULES
Image 1 shows the main view of ONE product. Images 2 onward
are additional views of that same item, possibly with different lighting or backgrounds.
If the main view is a close-up or label, use the most complete view for the full
silhouette and pose; close-ups supply details, not the output framing.
Use them to resolve construction, straps, closures and graphic details. Do not move
back-only details onto the front or average lighting casts across photos. Keep one
consistent product; never make a collage, combine items or reproduce props.
Every view depicts the SAME physical instance. Reconstruct it once; do not render
one garment for each reference or put a plain copy behind the detailed product.

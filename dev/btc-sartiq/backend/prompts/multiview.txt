MULTI-VIEW REFERENCE RULES
The first TWO images are the isolated primary product: detail crop and full framing.
Images 3 onward are additional photographs of that SAME physical product, not
additional products to generate. They may show details, another angle or the back,
and may include room backgrounds, hangers, hands or unrelated worn clothing.
These rules extend the two-reference instructions below.

Generate exactly ONE complete product in the required catalogue pose, never a
collage, contact sheet, multiple copies or a combination of different garments.
The primary references establish the selected target, visible side, overall design
and color palette after lighting assessment. Additional views clarify construction,
straps, ties, closures, authentic proportions, prints and legible lettering. Restore
visible product parts omitted by the primary cutout when supported by these views,
especially thin shoulder straps and their attachment points. Discard only props.
Do not transfer graphics, pockets or lettering from the back onto the front, or
invent an unseen front design. Distinguish viewpoints rather than merging them.

Additional photographs may have different lighting and have NOT been white-balanced.
Use their geometry and details; do not average their casts or overwrite the primary
palette with yellow, blue or other room lighting. Preserve intentional cream shades.
Never reproduce their background, pose, camera tilt, wrinkles, hanger or another
person's clothing. When views conflict, prefer the primary product evidence and
do not fabricate hybrid details. All pressing and front-view styling rules still apply.

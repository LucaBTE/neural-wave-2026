Describe this selected product for a catalogue photographer. Do not assume clothing.
Analyze only the selected isolated product, originally centered in the source photo.

that garment, not the person. Ignore hands, hangers and OTHER peripheral clothing.

neckline, sleeves, cuffs, hem, closures, pockets, knit/weave and graphic placement.

outfit, including their color, width and attachment. State the pattern's geometry,

Transcribe only clearly legible text; mark unclear lettering unreadable.

design features. Distinguish structural sewn pleats from accidental creases.
